The pipeline

Four stages between your prompt and your image

The same system design frontier labs use — planning, search, judging, repair — running on every Studio-mode generation.

Plan

A language model expands your prompt into a full scene spec — composition, lighting, palette — plus a checklist the image must satisfy.

Search

Multiple candidates are rendered from different interpretations, because the best image is rarely the first draw.

Judge

A vision model scores every candidate against the checklist — fidelity first, aesthetics second — and picks the winner.

Repair

Remaining defects are named, fixed with a refinement pass, and re-judged. Only the better result ships.
