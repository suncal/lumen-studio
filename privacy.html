Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

What we collect

The minimum needed to run the service: your access key, remaining credit balance, the prompts you submit, and the images generated from them. Job records (prompt + result) are kept for 24 hours to let you fetch your images, then expire. Payments are processed by our checkout provider; we never see or store your card details.

What we don't do

We don't require an account, don't track you across other sites, don't sell or share your data with advertisers, and don't use your private prompts or images for marketing without asking you first.

Where things run

Generation runs on cloud GPU infrastructure; prompts and outputs pass through those providers only as needed to produce your image. The Studio web app stores your key and gallery history in your own browser's local storage — clearing site data removes them.

Model improvement

Studio-mode generations produce anonymous quality scores (which candidate was better) used to improve the engine. These contain prompts and images but are never linked to you beyond the key's credit accounting.
